Application Guide

How to Use EPOREX Table Top Resin

Here is how you can achieve professional results through simple steps:

  1. Make sure that the surface is clean and dry without dust and oils.
  2. Measure the amount of resin and hardener by following the required ratio of mixing which depends on the type of resin you use. It will be 2:1 or 1:1.
  3. Stir the mixture for 3-5 minutes to ensure thorough mixing.
  4. Apply the resin on the surface.
  5. Bake the resin without leaving any air bubbles.
  6. Wait until it hardens before handling the project.
  7. Get your very clear, strong, and shiny finish.
